## LinkedIn (truck owners and fleet operators)

Fewer reported truck hijackings doesn't necessarily mean fewer hijackings.

SAPS figures reported by FreightNews show 327 truck hijackings between April and June 2026, down from 414 a year earlier. But the Road Freight Association warns the drop may reflect under-reporting rather than less cargo crime. Gauteng and KwaZulu-Natal remain hotspots.

With the festive peak putting more loads on the road, a few basics for owners and small fleets:
→ Brief drivers on what to do (and not do) in a hijacking
→ Agree check-in points on long routes
→ Keep load and route details off social media
→ Report every incident, so hotspots can be tracked
