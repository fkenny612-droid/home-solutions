## LinkedIn (primary channel) ⭐ best post

Asset registers shouldn't be rebuilt by consultants every audit cycle.

In June, the Auditor-General reported that municipalities made limited progress on audit outcomes in 2024-25, with regressions at several metros. National Treasury's MFMA compliance report also looks closely at municipal asset management, including how many municipalities still rely on consultants for it.

Registers that are only brought up to date for the audit tend to drift again straight after it. What helps:
→ Recording acquisitions, transfers and disposals when they happen
→ Regular verification by the people who use the assets
→ One system that finance, asset management and IT all work from
→ An audit trail that's ready before the auditors ask

AssetTrack ZA is a multi-tenant SaaS platform for tracking equipment, IT and fixed assets, built to keep registers accurate all year, so audit season is about checking, not rebuilding.
