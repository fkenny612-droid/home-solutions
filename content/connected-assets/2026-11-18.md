## LinkedIn (primary channel) ⭐ best post

On the last day before the builders' break, can you say where every drill, generator and compactor is?

For contractors and plant hire companies, tools and equipment move every day — between sites, vans, subcontractors and the yard. By December, the paper sign-out sheet rarely matches reality.

Insurers' loss-prevention guidance is clear: tool theft is a significant risk, and high-value tools and plant need documented controls. A simple pre-break routine helps:
→ Check every high-value tool and machine back in, or record exactly where it's staying
→ Note who signed out anything that isn't back
→ Lock up and record what's on each site
→ Start January with a list, not a search

AssetTrack ZA tracks equipment and fixed assets across sites, vehicles and people, with check-out and check-in that takes seconds.
