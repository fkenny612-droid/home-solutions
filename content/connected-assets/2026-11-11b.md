## LinkedIn (primary channel)

Earlier this year, Daily Maverick reported that the Auditor-General could not find assets at the locations recorded in a national department's asset register.

It's an extreme case, but the lesson applies to every organisation: an asset register is only as reliable as the last time someone checked it.

Most companies still verify assets once a year, on a spreadsheet, just before the auditors arrive. In between, laptops change hands, equipment moves between sites and things quietly disappear.

Continuous tracking changes that:
→ Every check-out and transfer is recorded when it happens
→ Missing or idle assets show up early, not at year-end
→ Verification becomes a confirmation, not a search
