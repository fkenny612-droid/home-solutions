## LinkedIn (primary channel) ⭐ best post

For event hire companies, December is a stock-take that never stops.

Marquees, chairs, tables, linen, décor, lighting and sound go out to year-end functions and weddings on Friday and come back on Monday, often from several events at once. By January, it's hard to say what went missing, what came back damaged, and which client had it.

What helps through the season:
→ Every item or batch tagged and counted out per event
→ Counted back in on return, with damage noted straight away
→ A clear record of which client and which venue had what
→ A January report on losses and repairs, not a guess
