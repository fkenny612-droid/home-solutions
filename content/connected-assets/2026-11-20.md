## LinkedIn (primary channel) ⭐ best post

Roughly 19,200 tablets. About R159 million. Unaccounted for.

In August, Daily Maverick reported that only about 64% of tablets in the Eastern Cape's matric tablet programme came back at the end of 2025. It's an expensive lesson for every school, district and organisation that lends devices: handing devices out is the easy part. Getting them back, and knowing who has what, is the hard part.

As the school year ends, a few basics:
→ A record of every device: tag, serial number and who it was issued to
→ A return list per class or department, checked off as devices come back
→ Follow-up on anything outstanding before learners and staff leave
→ Devices stored securely over the holidays, with a record of where

AssetTrack ZA tracks equipment and IT assets, including who has each device and where it is, so the end-of-year return is a checklist, not a mystery.
