The best time to service equipment is when nobody needs it.

For many businesses, that's the December shutdown. Aircons, generators, compressors, forklifts, vehicles and machines can be serviced without stopping work — if you know what's due.

Before you close:
→ List every asset that needs servicing or calibration
→ Check the last service date and what's due
→ Book suppliers for the shutdown window now
→ Record each service when it's done, ready for next time
