## LinkedIn (primary channel) ⭐ best post

If you insure R1 million of equipment for R600,000, a R100,000 claim may not pay R100,000.

That's the "average" clause: when the sum insured is less than the real value of what you own, many insurers reduce the claim in proportion, even on a partial loss. In this example, you could get around R60,000.

The root problem is usually simple: nobody knows exactly what the business owns, what it's worth today and where it is.

Before your next renewal:
→ Get a complete, current list of your equipment, IT and fixed assets
→ Record locations, condition and replacement values
→ Remove what you've sold or scrapped, add what you've bought
→ Go through the list with your broker

AssetTrack ZA keeps a live asset register, so renewals start from facts, not guesses.
