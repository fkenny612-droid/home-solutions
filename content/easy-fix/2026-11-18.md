## Facebook / Instagram — Variant 1 (carousel, when the water comes back) ⭐ best post

💧 Water back on after an outage? Check these 4 things:

1⃣ Open one cold tap first and let it run until the air and dirty water clear
2⃣ Listen for hissing or running water when every tap is closed
3⃣ Check under sinks and around the geyser for new drips
4⃣ Watch your water meter for an hour with everything off — if it moves, you may have a leak

Spotted a leak? Get a plumber in before it becomes a ceiling.
