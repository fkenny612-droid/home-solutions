## LinkedIn (service providers)

The busiest month for tradespeople is also the worst month to wait for money.

Business Partners warned earlier this year that late payment is a serious problem for South African SMEs, and that small businesses paid late often have to delay paying their own suppliers. For a plumber or electrician, that's materials, fuel and wages.

What helps most:
→ A written quote, with payment terms, before work starts
→ Deposits for materials on bigger jobs
→ An invoice on the day the job is finished
→ Easy ways to pay
