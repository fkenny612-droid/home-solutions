# Easy Fix — 2026-11-27 (Friday, Black Friday)

**Audience this run:** Homeowners
**Theme:** Bought an aircon, dishwasher or washing machine on Black Friday? Get it installed properly.
**Why now / research:** It's Black Friday, and big appliances need proper connections. Aircon refrigerant work should be done by a registered gas practitioner (SAQCC-Gas) who issues a Certificate of Conformity, and a new dedicated circuit needs a registered electrician who issues a Certificate of Compliance.
