## Facebook / Instagram — Variant 2 (questions to ask)

Before you say yes to a quote, ask these 4 things: 📝

✔️ Can I see a written quote with what's included?
✔️ Are you registered for this kind of work? (e.g. electricians should give you a Certificate of Compliance for electrical work)
✔️ Can I speak to someone you've worked for?
✔️ What deposit do you need, and what for?

A good pro won't mind you asking.

## Facebook / Instagram — Variant 3 (Reel, going away)

Going away for the holidays? 🧳 Do this before you lock up:

⚡ Switch off the geyser at the DB board
💧 Know where your water mains is (and show whoever is house-sitting)
🔌 Unplug what you don't need
📞 Tell your neighbour or security company you're away
