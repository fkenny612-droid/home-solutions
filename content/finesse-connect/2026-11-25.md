## LinkedIn

The riskiest key to your business might belong to someone who no longer works there.

Security commentators call them "shadow employees": former staff whose access was never fully removed. It usually happens because access sits with different people — HR collects the laptop, someone else handles the alarm code, and nobody deletes the fingerprint from the door reader.

Year-end is when contracts end, interns leave and resignations take effect, just before premises close for the holidays. A simple offboarding checklist for physical security:
→ Cancel access cards and tags, and remove biometrics
→ Change shared alarm and gate codes
→ Record returned keys and remotes
→ Review who can get in over the shutdown
